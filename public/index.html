How to read a card

Volume pace
Shares traded so far today vs. a normal day (20-day average) at the same point in the session. Above 1.5× means unusually heavy trading.
5-day
Price change over the last 5 trading days, the window the scorecard will judge signals on.
vs 20-day high
How far the price is below its highest point of the last 20 days. At or above 0% is a breakout.
